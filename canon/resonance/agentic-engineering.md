# Agentic Engineering (Karpathy) — Convergent Advice, Divergent Infrastructure

> Andrej Karpathy, 2025–2026. The convergence is directional validation at a visibility canon could not reach — and a gift of shared vocabulary; the divergence is the one the Shoemaker resonance revealed: agentic engineering is advice a human holds in their head, ODD is infrastructure a session runs on. After what Karpathy described as a December 2025 reliability inflection in coding agents, his January 2026 thread retired "vibe coding" (his own early-2025 coinage) for "agentic engineering" — human owns spec and plan, agent produces the change, verification is mandatory before "done" — which the community codified as five guidelines, each mapping onto ODD mechanics documented contemporaneously and derived from local pain (skimmed at release, deliberately set aside, studied properly July 2026; ambient exposure acknowledged). The page also proposes two Bend candidates for canon: the surgical-change constraint and the senior-engineer simplicity test. Sibling page: [AI Coding Toolkit — Harness Engineering](klappy://canon/resonance/ai-coding-toolkit).

## Summary — Convergence Validates; Divergence Is the Product

Karpathy's agentic engineering (January 2026) and ODD arrived at the same structural answers in the same window, from different pain. His three layers — spec, verify, environment — map onto exploration+planning, validation, and the canon substrate; his five codified guidelines each restate an ODD mechanic. That convergence, from one of the field's most visible voices, is directional validation of the load-bearing structures and a gift of market-tested vocabulary ODD could never have evangelized alone.

The divergence is the product: agentic engineering is advice a human holds in their head — no memory, no drift detection, no governed knowledge base, self-verification in the same session, coding-only scope. ODD is infrastructure a session runs on. Everyone now implements the advice as a one-off; ODD implements it as a reproducible substrate. Two Bend candidates are proposed for canon (surgical-change constraint; senior-engineer simplicity test), and a Bide row holds primary-source verification of quotes, dates, and the LLM knowledge base characterization, with a named tripwire.

## How This Was Found — Twice, With Deliberate Distance

Twice, in two different postures. First contact was at release: the January 2026 thread was a hot topic, and a skim showed surface alignment with what ODD was already doing and thinking — striking enough to be hard to believe. The deliberate response was to set it aside and keep plowing. Not obtuseness, and not cave-dwelling: in the age of AI every tweet self-proclaims "everything changes with this approach," and the only defense is to make sure everything built traces to observed pain and serves a purpose — not to what the leading personalities are saying. Adoption-by-celebrity is exactly the failure mode the axioms exist to prevent.

Second contact was on 2026-07-18, sitting down and actually listening: a video review of how Karpathy works in 2026, framing the method as three layers — spec, verify, environment — with embedded interview clips (Bee transcript on file). The same video series carries Boris Cherny's Claude Code workflows and Anthropic's skills guidance; those are candidate sibling resonance pages, not this one. The surface alignment held all the way down: the same structural answers ODD had been documenting since January, arrived at from different pain. As with the Shoemaker toolkit, recognition preceded comparison — and this two-contact shape is its own evidence. Six months of building *without* consulting the method, followed by deep convergence when finally compared, is a cleaner independence claim than never having seen it at all, because the non-adoption was a choice made on principle and the derivation continued from local evidence the whole time.

The reaction on second contact was discouragement — "everybody caught up; why build?" The resonance discipline exists to convert exactly that reaction into a map: name the alignment, name the divergence, decide what to borrow, and refocus on what nobody else carries.

## Timeline — Contemporaneous, Not Derivative (Either Direction)

*Dates below are per secondary coverage observed 2026-07-18; verify against primary threads before public-facing use.*

- **Early 2025** — Karpathy coins *vibe coding*: prompt, accept, recycle errors, don't read the code. This is the opposite of ODD's posture and is not the resonant artifact.
- **Mid 2025** — The Software 3.0 framing: natural language as the new programming interface. Direction-setting, still pre-discipline.
- **December 2025** — What Karpathy later called an agentic inflection point: generated code became reliable enough to delegate larger tasks, which made the failure modes of unsupervised delegation visible at scale.
- **January 2026** — The viral thread. Vibe coding declared passé; *agentic engineering* named as the successor. The community codifies five guidelines: surface assumptions and ambiguities before proceeding; apply a simplicity constraint; apply a surgical-change constraint; define verifiable success criteria before execution; execute, then verify with evidence.
- **January 2026** — ODD's founding documents begin in the same window. The thread is skimmed at release, surface alignment noted, deep study deliberately deferred.
- **July 2026** — Deep study; this resonance page.

The timeline matters for one epistemic reason: this is parallel discovery driven by the same December capability jump. Ambient exposure after January is acknowledged — the thread was skimmed, not studied — but the derivation continued from local pain, not from the method, and the deliberate choice not to adopt at peak hype is part of the record. If the dates survive primary-source verification, they are load-bearing evidence that the patterns are forced by the problem, not by fashion; until then they are working chronology.

## ODD Principle — Discipline Must Be Structural, Not Held in the Head

ODD encodes epistemic discipline as live infrastructure — modes with gates, claims with debts, validation with required artifacts — so that the discipline survives session boundaries, model swaps, and human forgetfulness. Advice that lives in a practitioner's habits does not compound and does not transfer.

## Where ODD Aligns — Spec, Verify, Environment Map to Modes, Validation, Canon

As framed by the reviewed video, the method's mature form resolves into three layers — spec, verify, environment — and each maps to ODD structure:

- **Spec layer** ≈ exploration + planning. Karpathy rejects plan mode as too shallow: work with the agent to design the actual spec. The goal-vs-task distinction — the goal is the decision the artifact drives, something the agent can never decide for you — is ODD's outcome-over-artifact axiom. Small, agile, tightly-scoped specs with review checkpoints ≈ mode gates with locked scope per pass.
- **Verify layer** ≈ validation as a mode. Evaluation criteria defined before the agent touches anything ≈ definition of done at the gate. A second model as critic ≈ community checking / cross-evaluator review. Pulling external signal to verify (check the actual deployment, not the agent's claim) ≈ "what I have not seen, I do not know" — evidence from reality, not self-report.
- **Environment layer** ≈ the canon substrate. His viral "LLM knowledge base" — a curated folder system of your own knowledge, structured for agent ingestion — is the field independently arriving at the knowledge-base layer canon has occupied since January. Hook-enforced guardrails — a rule in the prompt is a request; a pre-tool-use hook is a rule, as the reviewer put it — carry the same insight as governance enforced at the tool layer rather than the prompt layer. The always-do / ask-first / never-do buckets ≈ execution autonomy, gated transitions, and hard constraints.

The convergence, item by item — the five codified guidelines first, then the method's broader framing:

- **Simplicity constraint** ≈ scope discipline — strip unrequested features; the execution pass produces only what the locked scope names.

- **Surface assumptions and ambiguities before proceeding** ≈ exploration mode plus `oddkit_challenge` — assumptions are made visible and pressure-tested before intent narrows.
- **Verifiable success criteria before execution** ≈ the planning→execution gate and `oddkit_preflight` — definition of done is fixed before the artifact is produced.
- **Execute, then verify with evidence** ≈ "a claim is a debt" and `oddkit_validate` — VERIFIED or NEEDS_ARTIFACTS, never a bare "done."
- **Surgical-change constraint** ≈ scope lock after "go" — every changed line traces to the locked intent; no drive-by edits, no retroactive reframing.
- **Human owns spec and plan; agent owns production** (framing, not one of the five) ≈ the captain/first-officer division and mode discipline — judgment stays with the operator, artifacts come from the agent, gates sit between.
- **Supervisor posture over line-by-line review** (framing, not one of the five) ≈ bottleneck respect — operator attention is the constraint; the structure spends it at gates, not per line.

## Where ODD Diverges (Explicit) — Advice in the Head vs. Infrastructure in the Session

- **Advice vs. infrastructure.** The guidelines are held in a human's head and pasted into prompts. ODD's equivalent rules are fetched live from canon at the moment of use, versioned and hash-addressed. A guideline forgotten is a guideline gone; a canon rule forgotten is still enforced at the next gate.
- **No memory.** Agentic engineering has no mechanism for lessons to accumulate. ODD's debrief→canon loop compounds across projects, models, and sessions. The January thread itself will drift out of the field's working memory; canon does not.
- **Knowledge base as private folder vs. governed substrate.** The LLM knowledge base is the closest the method comes to canon, and the gap is the point: as described in the reviewed video (characterization not yet verified against Karpathy's own writeup — if his concept includes versioning or governance, this divergence weakens and must be revised), it is a folder on one machine, unversioned, un-addressed, with no disclosure contract, no drift detection, and no way for a second person or a fresh session to trust what is in it. Canon is URI-addressed, hash-verified, fetched live at the moment of use, and shared across every session and model that boards.
- **Coding-only.** The five guidelines govern software agents. ODD's modes, axioms, and validation govern any knowledge work — the same engine serves a theology corpus unchanged.
- **Output verification vs. outcome validation.** "Verify with evidence" asks whether the change did what the spec said. ODD additionally asks whether the spec matched reality, and names the further question — does the result transfer to the people it is for — as the community-checking method (canon records outcome validation as method; output validation is what is operational today). Drift detection (`canon/values/drift`, `docs/appendices/drift-checks`) has no equivalent in the guidelines.
- **Self-verification accepted.** The guidelines let the producing agent verify its own work in the same session. ODD requires a context break — the creator cannot be its own critic (`canon/principles/verification-requires-fresh-context`).
- **Implicit values.** The guidelines embody good judgment without articulating the values that generate it. ODD makes the values explicit, forkable, and load-bearing — which is what lets the discipline transfer to a stranger, or a model, without Karpathy in the room.

## Why the Divergence Matters — Advice Decays; the Vocabulary Is a Gift

The guidelines went out as advice at repost-count scale (per secondary coverage), and advice decays: it is applied unevenly, forgotten under deadline pressure, and never accumulates. Every team now adopting agentic engineering will shortly hit the failure modes the guidelines cannot address — silent drift between assumptions and reality, lessons relearned per project, correct output that was the wrong thing to build. Those failures live at ODD's layer. The field converging on the advice layer is the strongest signal yet that the infrastructure layer is where original work still pays.

The convergence is also a gift of vocabulary. Spec, verify, environment, agentic engineering — these words have now been run by a practitioner community orders of magnitude larger than canon's reach (tens of thousands of reposts on the January thread alone, per secondary coverage), who know the need and know from experience that the concepts are hard to implement. The most expensive part of introducing ODD — convincing anyone the problem exists — has been paid by someone else, at a reach canon could never buy. ODD can therefore be explained *from* this vocabulary rather than against it: each layer maps to an ODD mechanic, and ODD begins where the vocabulary runs out — everyone implements agentic engineering as a one-off, per person, per repo, per CLAUDE.md; ODD implements it as a reproducible substrate — versioned, shared, governed, compounding — per the substrate architecture canon already names (`canon/architecture/substrate-stack`). The positioning decision that follows from this is recorded in the session ledger of 2026-07-18, not here; this page is the anchor that records what the borrowed vocabulary meant when ODD hinged on it, so positioning drift stays detectable.

## 6B Operationalization — Borrow the Formulations, Bide on the Primary Sources, Build the Substrate

- **Borrow** — The five guidelines as prompt-layer patterns for coding agents; they are compatible with ODD and already largely restated by it. The maturing agentic-engineering tool ecosystem is Layer 4 material, per the sibling Shoemaker page.
- **Bend** — Two formulations are crisper than anything canon currently states and are candidates for adoption as named constraints with attribution: the *surgical-change constraint* (every changed line traces to the request) and the *senior-engineer simplicity test* (would a senior engineer call this overbuilt? — phrasing per the community codification). Subject to the 6B evaluation per `canon/constraints/borrow-evaluation-before-implementation` before any canon change.
- **Break** — No memory, no drift detection, no outcome validation, no context-broken verification, coding-only scope. These are not defects to fix in the method; they mark where the advice layer ends.
- **Bide** — Waiting, with tripwires: primary-source verification of the quotes, dates, and the LLM knowledge base writeup is deferred, not skipped. Tripwire: if Karpathy's own writeup shows the knowledge base concept includes versioning or governance, the knowledge-base divergence is revised and this page's stability claim re-evaluated.
- **Beget** — Let Karpathy and the community carry the popularization. The January thread evangelized spec-first and verify-before-done to an audience canon could never reach. That work no longer needs doing.
- **Build** — Only what the advice layer cannot carry: governance as runtime, the compounding ledger, domain-blind modes, outcome validation, drift detection. Same conclusion as the Shoemaker page, now confirmed from one of the most visible voices in the field: build only what nobody else can carry.
