# Epoch 12 — Orientation: The Record Is Placed in Time

> Epoch 10 gave the model a seat. Epoch 11 put the seat inside a loop and made the gate the verdict. Epoch 12 is the second O of OODA. Observability (E0008) gave us plenty to look at; what keeps failing is orientation, and specifically orientation backward. This morning a seat re-asked a question I had answered the night before, and reported a gate red that had been green for three and a half hours. Nothing was unobservable; the seat could not place what it held in time. So the unit of trust stays the loop, and what changes is what makes a status valid: every claim about state carries was, is and will be, each with its observed time, confidence, proximity and relevance. Orientation also means prior art: every plan names what was already tried, inside and outside the house, and when it was viable. Moving off the home-rolled harness onto plain GitHub repos is what makes this possible, one shared record any seat can board and orient in. Decide is probably next. Terry gives me hope a model can decide well from observed data, but Terry is stuck orienting in the documented timeline today, which is why this epoch comes first. Declared as an experiment, with retraction conditions on the record.

## Summary — Orientation Is the Center of Gravity

Boyd called orientation the schwerpunkt of the loop (`canon/resonance/ooda-loop`); I am paying for not taking him literally. E0008 made the system observable and E0011 made the loop the frame; neither said how a seat holds what it observed once the clock moves on. A status without a place in time is a mood; a reading without an observed time is a memory. Orientation keeps those apart, backward into the record and outward into what the world has tested.

## The Forcing Fault — Green for Three and a Half Hours

On 2026-10-09, around 09:30 ET, a seat reported a release gate red. It had seen red near 23:05 the night before; the gate had been green since 06:05. In the same stretch it re-asked a question the journal had answered hours earlier. Every sentence was once true. None was placed in time.

E0011 does not catch this. The loop was intact, the gate ran fresh, the receipts existed. The failure sits between gates: a token, "red", carried without a timestamp, aging silently in context (`canon/observations/time-blindness-axiom-violation`). One incident, but the shape of most friction logged since the loop landed.

## What Changes

The unit of trust stays the loop. What changes is what makes a status valid.

**Placed in time.** A reader who missed the last stretch can place every claim as was, is, or will be, each with its observed or expected time, confidence, proximity and relevance. Proximity is a last-mile rendering of a stored timestamp, never a stored "recent".

**Oriented against prior art.** A plan names the internal and external solutions already tested and when each was viable: the 6B table and the priorart skill, promoted from good practice to a condition the plan meets before it binds (`canon/methods/borrow-bend-break-beget-build`).

**One vocabulary.** Nine governance kinds, six consumers, one ledger (`canon/definitions/governance-artifacts`). A seat that cannot tell a principle from a mandate orients in neither.

**A record any harness can board.** ARS, the home-rolled harness, is on ice; its lessons carried forward in shape. The program lives in GitHub repos, files and folders now: one record, the same for every seat on every host.

## The Binding Contract

`status-carries-three-tenses` governs every status, handoff, release note, roadmap and agent-to-agent schema. `data-carries-observed-time` governs every datum a model reads, holds or reports. Together: observe before you state, stamp what you read, re-observe when the age exceeds how fast the thing changes, mark the untimed as untimed.

## The Mandatory Evidence

Observed times on every tense; a bare present-tense status is unproven. Prior art on every plan; the three bands and the 6B table on the record before a brief binds.

## What Does Not Change

The axioms, the creed, the canon corpus, the oddkit tools. Observability (E0008). The seat and boarding frame (E0010). The two-loop frame, `policy-precedes-build`, gate-passage receipts, fresh validation (E0011). This epoch adds a condition on top of the loop; nothing beneath it moves.

## Decide Is Next, Which Is Why This Comes First

Most decisions here have been mine. Terry, the law seat, is the first evidence a model can rule well from observed data and proper orientation, and Terry is blocked today on exactly that. No decide epoch while the seat that would prove it cannot place the record in time.

## Declared as an Experiment

Revert to E0011's frame, three tenses as recommended discipline rather than validity condition, if across a meaningful sample the stamping proves to be ceremony that does not reduce stale reports and re-asked questions, or if timing every datum costs more attention than it saves. Nothing below the condition is touched by that reversion.
